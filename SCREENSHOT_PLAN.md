# Neon Drift Dash Screenshot Plan

## 1. Home Screen With Logo

Goal: Establish the premium neon identity immediately.

Suggested caption: "Start the rooftop rush"

State needed in app: High score seeded or earned, selected board visible, logo centered.

Demo setup: Use the DEBUG `Home - Demo Ready` preview or play until a high score exists. Capture after the logo pulse settles and the selected board card is readable.

## 2. Active Gameplay With Combo/Shards

Goal: Show the actual arcade action: rider, hazards, shards, HUD, and neon parallax.

Suggested caption: "Chain shards. Keep the combo alive."

State needed in app: Mid-run with combo x3 or higher, at least one visible hazard, shields not empty.

Demo setup: Start a run, collect several shards, then capture while dragging near the lower-middle play area. Aim for a frame with score, combo, shields, and shards visible.

## 3. Wave Banner Moment

Goal: Communicate escalating difficulty and stylish wave pacing.

Suggested caption: "Survive the Neon Storm"

State needed in app: Wave 2, 3, or 4 transition banner visible.

Demo setup: Keep the run alive until the next wave transition. Capture during the banner plate animation with warning indicators or hazards entering.

## 4. Board Unlock / Progression Screen

Goal: Show replay goals and cosmetic progression.

Suggested caption: "Unlock boards with bigger runs"

State needed in app: Boards screen with at least Pulse/Nova unlocked and Phantom/Solar progress visible.

Demo setup: Use the DEBUG `Boards - Progression` preview or seed/earn a best score around 6,200. Capture with the current deck card and locked requirements visible.

## 5. Game Over / High Score Screen

Goal: Show the score chase and reward loop.

Suggested caption: "Run it back. Beat your best."

State needed in app: Game over overlay with final score, best score, shards, combo, and optionally a board unlock badge.

Demo setup: Use the DEBUG `Game Over - Unlock` preview or finish a run that crosses an unlock threshold. Capture after the game-over card animation finishes.
