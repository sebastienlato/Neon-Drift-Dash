## Description

Neon Drift Dash is a fast, stylish 2D arcade game built for quick reflexes and clean mobile runs.

Ride a futuristic hoverboard through a glowing rooftop city, weave around drone hazards and electric barriers, collect neon shards, and push your combo higher before the wave speed takes over. Every run ramps up with tighter traffic, sharper patterns, and bigger score pressure.

Unlock cosmetic boards by setting higher best scores:

- Pulse Board: unlocked from the start
- Nova Board: unlock at 2,500
- Phantom Board: unlock at 7,500
- Solar Board: unlock at 15,000

Features:

- Smooth drag-to-drift controls
- Neon rooftop parallax visuals
- Combo streak scoring
- Shield-based survival
- Increasing wave difficulty
- Local high score persistence
- Cosmetic board unlocks
- Sound and haptic toggles

No complicated setup. Start a run, chase the shard rush, and survive as long as you can.

## Age Rating Notes

Recommended for ages 13+. Gameplay is fast arcade dodging with non-realistic neon hazards. No realistic violence, no blood, no gambling, no user-generated content, and no social chat.

## Privacy Notes

Neon Drift Dash currently stores only local gameplay preferences and scores using on-device UserDefaults:

- High score
- Selected board
- Sound setting
- Haptics setting

No account system, no analytics SDK, no ads, no tracking, and no network gameplay are implemented.
