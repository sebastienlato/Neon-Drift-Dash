# Neon Drift Dash Image Asset Prompts

These prompts were used as the production direction for the generated assets in `Neon Drift Dash/Assets.xcassets`. Transparent gameplay assets were generated on a flat chroma-key background, then converted to alpha PNGs and sliced into individual image sets.

## Player Hoverboard Character

### PlayerIdle

Style: premium 2D mobile game art, cyberpunk rooftop arcade, clean readable silhouette, saturated neon rim lighting, sharp edges, soft glow, high detail, teenager appeal, not childish. Centered full-body character with generous padding, no text, no logo, no watermark.

For built-in generation with local extraction: put the sprite on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for background removal. The background must be one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the subject.
